When Can You Request a Return?

Incorrect Item Delivered

If you receive a medicine or product different from what you ordered, contact our support team.

Damaged, Expired or Defective Product

If your delivered product is damaged, expired or defective, you may submit a return or refund claim for review.

Important: Medicines must not be returned or resold if their safety, storage conditions or integrity cannot be verified.
