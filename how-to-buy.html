Buy exactly the size you needKa sayi daidai girman da kake buƙata

The same medicine can be sold as one piece, a strip, a pack or a box. Read this short guide once, and you will always know what you are ordering. Magani iri ɗaya na iya zuwa da guda ɗaya, ko layi, ko fakiti, ko akwati. Ka karanta wannan ɗan jagora sau ɗaya, kuma za ka san ainihin abin da kake yin oda.

What the words meanMa'anar kalmomin

PieceGuda ɗaya

One single item: one tablet, one capsule, one bottle or one bandage.Abu ɗaya kawai: kwaya ɗaya, kwalba ɗaya, ko bandeji ɗaya.

Choose this when you need only a few.Ka zaɓi wannan idan kana buƙatar kaɗan kawai.

StripLayi (Strip)

A sheet of tablets or capsules joined together, usually 10 pieces.Takardar kwayoyi ko kapsul da aka haɗa waje ɗaya, yawanci guda 10.

Good for one short treatment.Ya dace da magani na ɗan lokaci kaɗan.

Pack or boxFakiti ko akwati

Many strips or items packed together in one carton. This is the full size the maker sells.Layuka ko kayayyaki da yawa a cikin kwali ɗaya. Wannan shi ne cikakken girman da kamfani ke sayarwa.

Choose this to stock up or to pay less per piece.Ka zaɓi wannan idan kana son ajiya ko ka biya kuɗi kaɗan a kowace kwaya.

RollNadi (Roll)

Bandage, plaster or tape wound on a core. You buy the whole roll.Bandeji, filasta ko tef da aka naɗe. Ana sayen cikakken nadin.

If a product is sold by the roll, you cannot buy half of it.Idan ana sayar da abu da nadi, ba za a iya sayen rabin nadin ba.

How to read a pack sizeYadda ake karanta girman fakiti

A pack size such as 10x10 means 10 strips, with 10 tablets in each strip. Girman fakiti kamar 10x10 yana nufin layuka 10, kuma kowane layi yana da kwayoyi 10.

10 tablets=1 strip→10 strips=1 pack (100 tablets)
Kwayoyi 10=Layi 1→Layuka 10=Fakiti 1 (kwayoyi 100)

Before you orderKafin ka yi oda

  • Read the full name of the product. A size in brackets, such as (Big) or (Small), is a different size and may have a different price. Ka karanta cikakken sunan kayan. Girman da ke cikin baka, kamar (Big) ko (Small), girma ne daban kuma farashinsa na iya bambanta.
  • Open the product and check its strength and pack size. Ka buɗe kayan ka duba ƙarfin maganin da girman fakiti.
  • If the product does not say whether it is a piece, strip or pack, ask us first. We will gladly tell you. Idan kayan bai bayyana ko guda ɗaya ne, layi ne ko fakiti ba, ka tambaye mu tukuna. Za mu faɗa maka da farin ciki.
  • Some medicines need a doctor's prescription. Have it ready, and upload it if we ask. Wasu magunguna suna buƙatar takardar likita. Ka shirya ta, ka ɗora ta idan mun nema.
