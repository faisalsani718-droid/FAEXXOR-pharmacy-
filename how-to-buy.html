Buy exactly the size you needKa sayi daidai girman da kake buƙata

The same medicine can be sold as one piece, a strip, a pack or a box. Read this once and you will always know what you are ordering.Magani iri ɗaya na iya zuwa da guda ɗaya, ko layi, ko fakiti, ko akwati. Ka karanta wannan sau ɗaya, kuma za ka san ainihin abin da kake yin oda.

PieceGuda ɗaya

One single item: one tablet, one capsule, one bottle or one bandage.Abu ɗaya kawai: kwaya ɗaya, kwalba ɗaya, ko bandeji ɗaya.

Choose this when you need only a few.Ka zaɓi wannan idan kana buƙatar kaɗan kawai.

StripLayi (Strip)

A sheet of tablets or capsules joined together, usually 10 pieces.Takardar kwayoyi ko kapsul da aka haɗa waje ɗaya, yawanci guda 10.

Good for one short treatment.Ya dace da magani na ɗan lokaci kaɗan.

Pack or boxFakiti ko akwati

Many strips or items packed together in one carton. This is the full size the maker sells.Layuka ko kayayyaki da yawa a cikin kwali ɗaya. Wannan shi ne cikakken girman da kamfani ke sayarwa.

Choose this to stock up or to pay less per piece.Ka zaɓi wannan idan kana son ajiya ko ka biya kuɗi kaɗan a kowace kwaya.

RollNadi (Roll)

1 roll means 10 packets (or 10 bottles) of the same product, sold together as one roll.Nadi 1 yana nufin fakiti 10 (ko kwalabe 10) na kaya iri ɗaya, ana sayar da su tare a matsayin nadi ɗaya.

Example: 1 roll of syrup = 10 bottles.Misali: nadi 1 na syrup = kwalabe 10.

How to read a pack sizeYadda ake karanta girman fakiti

A pack size such as 10x10 means 10 strips, with 10 tablets in each strip.Girman fakiti kamar 10x10 yana nufin layuka 10, kuma kowane layi yana da kwayoyi 10.

10 tablets=1 strip→10 strips=1 pack (100 tablets)
Kwayoyi 10=Layi 1→Layuka 10=Fakiti 1 (kwayoyi 100)

How prices workYadda farashi ke aiki

1 piece price (normal price)Farashin guda 1 (na yau da kullum)

The price on the product is for 1 piece. If you buy 1 piece or only a few, you pay this price for each piece.Farashin da ke kan kayan na guda 1 ne. Idan ka sayi guda 1 ko kaɗan, za ka biya wannan farashi a kowace guda.

Supplier price (buy more, pay less)Farashin jumla (ka sayi da yawa, ka biya kaɗan)

Some products have a lower supplier price. When you reach the minimum shown, such as Buy 5 or more, every piece costs the lower price.Wasu kayayyaki suna da rahusan farashin jumla. Idan ka kai mafi ƙarancin adadin da aka nuna, kamar Ka sayi guda 5 ko fiye, kowace guda za ta yi ƙaramin farashi.

Example: a medicine costs ₦500 for 1 piece, and ₦300 each from 5 pieces.Misali: magani yana ₦500 a guda 1, kuma ₦300 a kowace guda daga guda 5.

4 pieces=4 × ₦500 = ₦2,000
Guda 4=4 × ₦500 = ₦2,000
5 pieces=5 × ₦300 = ₦1,500
Guda 5=5 × ₦300 = ₦1,500
  • You see the supplier price on the product page and in your checkout list. It applies by itself once your quantity reaches the minimum.Za ka ga farashin jumla a shafin kayan da kuma a lissafin biyan kuɗi. Yana aiki da kansa da zarar adadinka ya kai mafi ƙaranci.
  • The delivery fee is not included. We confirm it with you.Kuɗin kai kaya ba ya ciki. Za mu tabbatar maka da shi.
  • The price in your checkout list is checked again when you place your order, so you always pay the current price.Ana sake duba farashin da ke lissafin biyan kuɗi lokacin da ka yi odarka, don haka kullum za ka biya farashin yanzu.

Common questionsTambayoyin da ake yawan yi

Do I need an account to order?Ina buƙatar asusu don yin oda?
Yes. You log in so we can send the order to your account and you can track it. Your cart stays saved while you sign in.Eh. Za ka shiga domin mu aika odar zuwa asusunka kuma ka bi diddiginta. Kwandonka yana nan a ajiye yayin da kake shiga.
Why can I not see Cash on Delivery?Me ya sa ba na ganin Cash on Delivery?
Cash on Delivery is a one-time offer for each account. Once you have placed a Cash on Delivery order, it is hidden on your next orders. Choose Bank Transfer or Pay with OPay instead. These two are always available.Cash on Delivery tayi ce ta sau ɗaya ga kowane asusu. Da zarar ka yi odar Cash on Delivery, zai ɓoye a odarka ta gaba. Ka zaɓi tura kuɗi ta banki ko Pay with OPay maimakon haka. Waɗannan biyun kullum suna nan.
I paid with OPay but my order still says pending.Na biya da OPay amma odata har yanzu tana cewa pending.
Wait a moment. We confirm the payment with OPay automatically. Then open My Orders and tap Check payment status. Please do not pay again. If it does not change, message us on WhatsApp with your order number.Ka jira ɗan lokaci. Muna tabbatar da biyan da OPay kai tsaye. Sannan ka buɗe My Orders ka danna Check payment status. Don Allah kar ka sake biya. Idan bai canza ba, ka aiko mana saƙo a WhatsApp tare da lambar odarka.
Is it safe to pay with OPay?Biya da OPay lafiya ne?
You pay on OPay's own secure page. We never see or ask for your card details, PIN or OTP. An order is marked paid only after OPay confirms the payment to us.Kana biya ne a shafin OPay amintacce. Ba mu ganin ko neman bayanan katinka, PIN ko OTP. Ana sa oda a matsayin an biya ne kawai bayan OPay ya tabbatar mana da biyan.
How much is delivery, and where do you deliver?Nawa ne kuɗin kai kaya, kuma ina kuke kai kaya?
We deliver to Yola, Gombe, Borno, Taraba and nearby locations. The fee depends on your location and is not included in the total shown. We confirm it with you.Muna kai kaya Yola, Gombe, Borno, Taraba da wuraren da ke kusa. Kuɗin ya danganta da inda kake kuma ba ya cikin jimillar da aka nuna. Za mu tabbatar maka da shi.
