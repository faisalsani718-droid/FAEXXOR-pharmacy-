1. Who we are

FX PHARMARKET is an online medicine marketplace operating in Nigeria. For the purposes of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), FX PHARMARKET is the data controller responsible for the personal information described in this policy. Contact details are in section 17.

2. Information we collect

A. Personal information

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Delivery address
  • Account login information (your password is stored in protected form)

B. Order information

  • Medicines and products you order
  • Order history, order numbers and transaction references
  • Delivery and order status

C. Prescription and health-related information

If you upload a prescription, we collect the document and the information in it (for example, your name, the medicine prescribed and the prescriber's details) so we can verify and process your request. This is sensitive personal information and receives extra protection.

D. Payment information

Payments are processed by third-party providers such as Paystack or other supported payment services. We do not store your full card details. We never ask you to share your ATM PIN, banking password or payment OTP with FX PHARMARKET. If anyone claiming to be us asks for these, do not share them and contact us immediately.

E. Technical information

  • Device and browser type, IP address and approximate location
  • Pages visited and how you use the website
  • Information gathered through cookies (see section 8)

F. Communications

Messages you send us through WhatsApp, phone, email or the website, so we can help with your orders and complaints.

3. How we use your information

  • Create and manage customer accounts
  • Process and confirm orders
  • Verify uploaded prescriptions where applicable
  • Contact you about your orders
  • Arrange product delivery
  • Process payments and refunds
  • Provide customer support and handle complaints
  • Improve our website and services
  • Detect and prevent fraud and unauthorized transactions
  • Send important service notifications
  • Meet legal and regulatory obligations

We use personal information only for legitimate business and service purposes.

4. Why we are allowed to use your information

Under the NDPA, we must have a lawful basis for using your information. We rely on:

  • Contract: to process your order and deliver it to you.
  • Consent: for example, when you upload a prescription, or agree to receive promotional messages. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep records or share information.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep our website secure, prevent fraud and improve our services, where this does not override your rights.

5. How we protect your information

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss or destruction, theft, unauthorized disclosure and misuse. These include secure (HTTPS) connections, restricted access to authorized personnel only, and requiring our service providers to protect your data.

No online platform can guarantee 100% security. Please keep your password private and log out on shared devices.

6. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share only what is necessary with:

  • Payment providers, to process transactions
  • Delivery companies, to deliver your orders
  • Licensed pharmacies and marketplace vendors fulfilling your order
  • Technical service providers who help operate our website
  • Government or regulatory authorities, where required by law or a valid legal request

We expect all service providers to handle your information responsibly, securely and only for the purpose we shared it.

7. Prescription and medical information

Your prescription information is private. If you upload a prescription:

  • Authorized personnel, including pharmacists, may review it to process your request.
  • Relevant details may be shared with the licensed pharmacy responsible for fulfilling your order.
  • It will not be published publicly.
  • We will not use it for unrelated advertising.

Only upload prescriptions that belong to you or that you are authorized to submit. We may decline to fill an order if a prescription cannot be verified.

FX PHARMARKET provides a marketplace for ordering medicines. Information on this website is not a substitute for advice from a doctor or pharmacist.

8. Cookies and website technology

We may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, keep you logged in, improve website performance and understand how visitors use the site. You can manage or disable cookies in your browser settings, although some features may not work properly.

9. Data retention

We keep your information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain order records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations and protect our business. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or securely dispose of it, subject to legal requirements.

Type of informationHow long we keep it
Account informationWhile your account is active, and until you ask us to delete it
Order and payment recordsAs long as needed for accounting, tax and legal purposes
Uploaded prescriptionsAs long as needed to fulfil your order and meet pharmacy record-keeping requirements
Support messagesAs long as needed to resolve your request and any follow-up

10. Your privacy rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable laws, you may have the right to:

  • Ask for access to your personal information
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information where the law permits
  • Object to or ask us to restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Receive a copy of your information in a commonly used format
  • Complain about how your information is handled

To use these rights, contact us using the details in section 17. We may need to confirm your identity first. We aim to respond within a reasonable time and within the period required by law. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

11. Messages and marketing

We will send you service messages about your orders, deliveries and account. We will send promotional messages only if you agree to them, and you can opt out at any time by contacting us or following the opt-out instructions in the message.

12. Transfers outside Nigeria

Some of our service providers (for example, website hosting or payment services) may process information outside Nigeria. Where this happens, we take steps to make sure your information remains protected as required by the NDPA.

13. Third-party links and services

Our website may link to third-party websites, payment platforms or other services. FX PHARMARKET is not responsible for their privacy practices. Please read their privacy policies before giving them personal information.

14. Children's privacy

Our services are not intended for children to create accounts or buy medicines on their own. Parents or legal guardians should supervise children's use of our services and the submission of their personal or medical information. If we learn that a child's information was collected without proper adult involvement, we will take steps to delete it.

15. Data breaches

If a security incident affects your personal information and creates a risk to your rights or safety, we will take steps to contain it and will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission and affected customers as required by law.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our services, technology or the law. Updates will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. For significant changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify you. Please review this page regularly.
